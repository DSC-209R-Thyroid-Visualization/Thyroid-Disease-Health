Chasing the Butterfly

One patient's path through Graves' treatment, draw by draw

Methimazole Methimazole is an antithyroid intervention I have been on for over two years. Other possible interventions include radioactive iodine, or a thyroidectomy for surgical removal.
Why "the butterfly"? The thyroid has two lobes, one on each side of the windpipe, joined by a thin bridge called the isthmus. Spread across the front of the neck, they look like a butterfly's wings. It weighs only about 20 grams, yet its hormones set the pace of nearly every cell in the body.
